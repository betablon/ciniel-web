2. What Ciniel is

Ciniel is a personal film journal. You can log films with dates, ratings and reviews, keep a watchlist, plan cinema screenings, browse and search films, and import your history from Trakt or Letterboxd. Film information comes from TMDB. Ciniel has no user accounts; your journal is stored on your device, as described in the Privacy Policy. Features can change between versions within the limits of section 9; the App Store listing describes the current version.

3. Ciniel Free

  • You can log 20 films yourself for free. A film counts the first time you log it; logging another watch of a film already in your journal costs nothing. Deleting a film doesn’t return it to your free allowance, and logging it again after deleting it counts as a new log. Films you log while you have Pro count too. Films you import, and adding films to your watchlist, don’t count.
  • You can import once for free from each supported source: currently Trakt, from the username of a public profile, and Letterboxd, from its export file. An import counts once it has completed; one that fails or that you cancel doesn’t.
  • The app shows how many free logs you have left.
