The interface

No two films look the same.

Ciniel reads the palette out of a film's own artwork and tints everything around it — the buttons, the badges, the ground it sits on. The app doesn't have one look. It has as many as your library does.

Three screens from the same build, on the same evening. The colour is the film's, not the app's.

One film, top to bottom

Every film brings its own colour.

Ciniel reads the palette out of the artwork and tints the whole page to it — the buttons, the rating badge, the ground it all sits on.

Everything you'd otherwise go looking for.

Cast and crew, runtime, release, the tagline, the collection it belongs to. One page, no tab hunting, and fetched once rather than every time you open it.

Every time you've seen it.
